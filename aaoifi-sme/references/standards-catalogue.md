# AAOIFI Standards Catalogue

As of the 45th AAB meeting (January 2026) and AGEB recent issuances, AAOIFI has issued **117+ standards** comprising:

- **33** Financial Accounting Standards (FAS) — current numbering through FAS 53
- **59** Shari'ah Standards (SS) — draft English translation through SS 61 (open for comment to 30 June 2026)
- **8** Auditing Standards for Islamic Financial Institutions (ASIFI)
- **14** Governance Standards for Islamic Financial Institutions (GSIFI)
- **3** Codes of Ethics

The official catalogue lives at https://aaoifi.com/e-standards/ (free access since 2024). This file gives you the working list and the cross-references most often needed.

> **Verification note.** Always cross-check standard numbers and titles against the AAOIFI website before issuing a deliverable. Some FAS have been superseded (notably FAS 17 by FAS 28; FAS 8 by FAS 32; FAS 18 by FAS 30/35); always cite the *current* operative standard.
