# AAOIFI Audit, Governance and Ethics — Reference

This file covers the three AAOIFI standard families that govern **how an IFI is audited, governed and behaves ethically** — the ASIFIs (Auditing), the GSIFIs (Governance), and the Code of Ethics. These standards are issued by the **AAOIFI Governance and Ethics Board (AGEB)**.

> The accounting standards tell you what to book; these standards tell you who watches over the bookings, the products, and the people.

## 1. The AAOIFI three-line-of-defence model for IFIs

AAOIFI's governance architecture maps onto the **classic three-line-of-defence (3LoD) model**, with a Shari'ah overlay:

| Line | Conventional 3LoD | IFI overlay (AAOIFI) |
|---|---|---|
| **First line** | Business / operations | Same — staff, branch, treasury, product teams |
| **Second line** | Risk, Compliance | Risk, Compliance **plus Shari'ah Compliance Function (GSIFI 9)** |
| **Third line** | Internal Audit | Internal Audit **plus Internal Shari'ah Audit (GSIFI 11)** |
| **Oversight** | Audit Committee | Audit Committee + **Shari'ah Supervisory Board (GSIFI 1)** + Audit & Governance Committee (GSIFI 4) |
| **External** | External Auditor | External Auditor **+ External Shari'ah Audit (ASIFI 6)** |

The Shari'ah leg is independent of the conventional risk and audit leg. In a well-governed IFI, the SSB reports to the Board of Directors directly, not via management.

## 2. ASIFI 1–8 — Auditing standards

Note: these complement (not replace) **International Standards on Auditing (ISA)** as adapted for IFIs.

| Standard | Coverage |
|---|---|
| **ASIFI 1** | Objective and Principles of Auditing — the umbrella |
| **ASIFI 2** | The Auditor's Report — required elements, opinion forms, comparative IFRS-vs-AAOIFI reporting |
| **ASIFI 3** | Terms of Audit Engagement — engagement letter, scope, fee, SSB liaison |
| **ASIFI 4** | Testing for Compliance with Shari'ah Rules and Principles by an External Auditor — the auditor's responsibility to test sample transactions for Shari'ah compliance |
| **ASIFI 5** | The Auditor's Responsibility to Consider Fraud and Error in an Audit of Financial Statements |
| **ASIFI 6** | External Shari'ah Audit (Independent Assurance Engagement) — the most-cited modern ASIFI; defines the External Shari'ah Audit (ESA) as a separate engagement from the financial-statement audit |
| **ASIFI 7** | Engagement for Performing Agreed-upon Procedures Regarding Zakah |
| **ASIFI 8** | Assurance Engagements other than Audits |

### ASIFI 6 — External Shari'ah Audit

ESA is increasingly **mandatory** in major jurisdictions:
- **Pakistan**: SBP requires ESA for Islamic banks and Islamic windows under the Shariah Governance Framework 2018.
- **Bahrain**: CBB Rulebook Volume 2 Module HC requires annual ESA.
- **UAE**: HSA-driven ESA framework being phased in.

The ESA report is **separate from** the financial-statement audit report — both are presented to shareholders. The ESA gives **reasonable assurance** that the IFI has complied with Shari'ah rules during the period.

## 3. GSIFI 1–14 — Governance standards

### Earlier GSIFIs (1–7)

| Standard | Topic |
|---|---|
| GSIFI 1 | SSB Appointment, Composition and Report |
| GSIFI 2 | Shari'ah Review (the SSB's ongoing review of operations) |
| GSIFI 3 | Internal Shari'ah Review (the predecessor of GSIFI 11) |
| GSIFI 4 | Audit and Governance Committee for IFIs |
| GSIFI 5 | Independence of the Shari'ah Supervisory Board |
| GSIFI 6 | Statement on Governance Principles for IFIs |
| GSIFI 7 | Corporate Social Responsibility, Conduct and Disclosure |

### Modern GSIFIs (8–14)

| Standard | Topic |
|---|---|
| **GSIFI 8** | **Central Shari'ah Board** — the 100th AAOIFI standard. Defines structure, mandate, fit-and-proper, secretariat for a Central Shari'ah Board operating at a national or regulator level |
| **GSIFI 9** | **Shari'ah Compliance Function** — the second-line-of-defence Shari'ah function inside an IFI |
| **GSIFI 10** | **Shari'ah Compliance and Fiduciary Ratings for IFIs** — the ratings methodology used by IIRA and similar agencies |
| **GSIFI 11** | **Internal Shari'ah Audit for IFIs** — the third-line-of-defence ISA function; the IFI equivalent of an internal-audit charter |
| **GSIFI 12** | **Sukuk Governance** — the governance of Sukuk issuer, trustee, sukuk-holder relations throughout the life of the issue |
| **GSIFI 13** | (in issuance / ED status — verify against latest AAOIFI announcement) |
| **GSIFI 14** | (in issuance / ED status — verify against latest AAOIFI announcement) |

### GSIFI 1 — SSB Composition

- Minimum **3 scholars**, all of whom must hold qualifications acceptable to the SSB (typically a doctorate or equivalent in Islamic jurisprudence — Fiqh al-Muamalat).
- At least one member must have **practical Islamic-finance experience**.
- Members appointed by the **shareholders in AGM** (or by the board with later shareholder ratification, depending on jurisdiction).
- **Term of office** — generally 3 years renewable.
- **No conflict of interest** — a scholar serving on more than a small number of SSBs may compromise independence (GSIFI 5).

### GSIFI 5 — Independence

- The SSB cannot be employees of the IFI.
- Their remuneration is fixed at the AGM, not by management.
- They report directly to the Board, not to the CEO.

## 4. The Codes of Ethics

Three codes are issued:

| Code | Coverage |
|---|---|
| **Code of Ethics for Accountants and Auditors of IFIs** | The professional code for accountants, internal auditors, external auditors working with IFIs. Builds on IFAC's IESBA Code with IFI overlays — e.g., explicit prohibition on accepting hospitality or gifts that may compromise independence in the IFI context |
| **Code of Ethics for Employees of IFIs** | The staff conduct code — confidentiality, no riba personal practice, avoidance of conflict of interest, dress and behaviour expectations |
| **Code of Ethics for Islamic Finance Professionals** | The professional-body code — relevant for IFI bodies awarding designations, including the **Credit Institute of Pakistan (CIP) Certified Credit Professional designation** in any Islamic-finance specialism |

These codes are foundational reference material when:
- Drafting an IFI's internal code of conduct
- Producing an SSB charter
- Writing an internal-audit fraud / ethics policy
- Designing a curriculum (e.g., CIP, NIBAF, IBA-CEIF) — codes are typically a required reading

## 5. The Shari'ah Supervisory Board — composition, charter, independence

A practical checklist when reviewing or drafting an SSB charter:

- [ ] Minimum 3 scholars named (GSIFI 1)
- [ ] Each scholar's qualifications and experience disclosed
- [ ] Appointment process — AGM resolution (GSIFI 1)
- [ ] Term, renewal, removal procedures
- [ ] Remuneration — fixed by AGM, disclosed in FS (GSIFI 5)
- [ ] Conflict-of-interest declarations annually
- [ ] Independence from management — direct reporting line to Board
- [ ] Mandate — pre-approval of new products (Shari'ah due diligence), ongoing Shari'ah review (GSIFI 2), annual Shari'ah audit (GSIFI 11 driving)
- [ ] Powers — to require information, suspend products, escalate to Board
- [ ] Secretariat / Shari'ah Compliance Function support (GSIFI 9)
- [ ] Annual Shari'ah audit plan approval
- [ ] Annual Shari'ah report in the financial statements (GSIFI 1, FAS 1)

## 6. The Central Shari'ah Board model (GSIFI 8)

GSIFI 8 — the **100th AAOIFI standard** — codifies the regulator-level **Central Shari'ah Board (CSB)** model. The CSB is a national body (typically housed under the central bank or a Shari'ah council) that:

- Issues binding fatwas at the system level on contested products
- Reviews IFI-level SSB fatwas for systemic consistency
- Approves national-level Sukuk programmes
- Oversees Shari'ah qualifications for IFI scholars

### Jurisdictional implementations

| Country | CSB / equivalent | Status |
|---|---|---|
| UAE | Higher Shariah Authority (HSA) | Operational since 2018 |
| Pakistan | Shariah Advisory Committee (SBP) | Established since the early 1980s; periodically restructured |
| Malaysia | Shariah Advisory Council (BNM) | Operational; binding under IFSA 2013 |
| Saudi Arabia | Shariah Committee at SAMA | Phased rollout |
| Indonesia | Dewan Syariah Nasional (DSN-MUI) | Operational since 1999 |
| Bahrain | Shariah Coordination Committee at CBB | Coordinating role |
| Oman | High Shari'ah Supervisory Authority | Operational |

In jurisdictions with a CSB, an IFI's SSB does not have unilateral authority — its fatwas may need ratification or are bounded by the CSB's framework.

## 7. Internal Shari'ah Audit (GSIFI 11) vs. External Shari'ah Audit (ASIFI 6)

| Dimension | Internal Shari'ah Audit (GSIFI 11) | External Shari'ah Audit (ASIFI 6) |
|---|---|---|
| Function | Third line of defence, inside the IFI | Independent assurance, from outside |
| Reports to | Audit Committee / SSB / Board (independent of management) | Shareholders, via Board and SSB |
| Frequency | Continuous (annual audit plan, quarterly reporting) | Annual |
| Scope | All operations from a Shari'ah-compliance perspective — products, transactions, charity fund, IAH disclosure, SSB recommendations follow-up | A defined scope agreed at engagement; typically opinion on Shari'ah compliance of operations for the period |
| Assurance level | Reasonable assurance (broad-scope continuous) | Reasonable assurance (point-in-time annual) |
| Output | Internal Shari'ah audit report to Board / Audit Committee / SSB | External Shari'ah Audit Opinion published in the annual report |
| Standards | GSIFI 11 (+ IIA standards adapted) | ASIFI 6 (+ ISAE 3000 adapted) |

The two functions must not be performed by the same person/entity (independence requirement).

## 8. Sukuk governance throughout the life of the issue (GSIFI 12)

GSIFI 12 codifies the governance of Sukuk from issuance to maturity, addressing the **gaps** that emerged after the 2008 declaration and the 2017 Dana Gas controversy.

### Issuance-stage requirements

- Pre-issuance Shari'ah approval from the issuer's SSB **and** the lead manager's SSB (if different)
- Sukuk prospectus to disclose:
  - Underlying assets/contracts and their classification (Ijarah / Mudaraba / Wakala / Hybrid)
  - The 51% tangible-asset rule compliance status (SS 17, SS 59 cross-reference)
  - The redemption mechanism (par for debt-type, NAV/market for equity-type)
  - The Shari'ah governance framework throughout the life
- Trustee / SPV Shari'ah due diligence

### Ongoing governance

- Periodic SSB review of:
  - Maintenance of the underlying asset pool
  - Distribution mechanics
  - Any restructuring, deferral, or default events
- Annual report to Sukuk-holders on Shari'ah compliance status
- Tradeability assessment — if the tangible-asset ratio falls below threshold, **the Sukuk may become non-tradable until restored**

### Restructuring and default

GSIFI 12 codifies how:
- A Sukuk restructuring requires SSB approval of the new terms
- A purchase undertaking can only be exercised at fair market value (Mudaraba/Musharakah)
- Sukuk-holder voting thresholds for material changes must respect Shari'ah principles (e.g., no acceptance of riba-based bridge financing as part of the restructure)

## 9. Shari'ah non-compliance income (SNCI) — treatment

When the IFI inadvertently or unavoidably earns income that the SSB determines is not Shari'ah-compliant (e.g., late-payment penalties, interest on a regulatorily mandated deposit at a conventional central bank, dividends from a screened equity that crosses purification thresholds):

1. **Recognise as a separate liability — "Income to be donated to charity"** — not as IFI income.
2. **Present in the Statement of Sources and Uses of Charity Funds** (FAS 1 Revised).
3. **Distribute to bona-fide charitable causes** under SSB oversight — the IFI does not retain economic benefit.
4. **Disclose annually** — composition, sources, distribution.

This is a recurring audit point — many IFIs route late-payment fees through P&L by oversight or by policy interpretation. Cleanup typically involves a multi-year reclassification.

## 10. SSB report template and required elements

The SSB Report (annually in the financial statements, per GSIFI 1) must contain:

1. **Addressee** — typically the shareholders.
2. **Scope statement** — period covered, basis (review of transactions, contracts, products).
3. **SSB's responsibility** vs. management's responsibility — clearly delineated.
4. **Work performed** — sampling approach, areas covered, reliance on Internal Shari'ah Audit.
5. **Opinion** — typically one of four forms:
   - **Unqualified** — the IFI operated in accordance with Shari'ah rules and principles during the period
   - **Qualified** — broadly compliant, with specific exceptions noted (typically with SNCI quantum)
   - **Adverse** — material non-compliance — rare, escalatory
   - **Disclaimer** — insufficient evidence — escalatory
6. **Recommendations** — for improvements in product structuring, controls, disclosure.
7. **Zakah computation confirmation** (cross-reference FAS 39, SS 35).
8. **Signatures** of SSB members and date.

This template is what you'd find in any AAOIFI-adopting IFI's annual report — useful to compare across jurisdictions when benchmarking.

---

> **Closing note.** When working on Shari'ah audit, governance, or ethics matters, always pair the standard with the IFI's actual SSB charter and the regulator's applicable framework. The standards set the floor; jurisdictions and individual IFIs often go further.
